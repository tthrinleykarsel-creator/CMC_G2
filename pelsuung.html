About Pelsung

Pelsung is a youth-driven initiative under GMC Business Startup Hub focused on creating opportunities, innovation, and leadership for Bhutanese youth.

In the past half-century, Bhutan grew from having the world's lowest GDP to having one of the highest per-capita GDP levels in the region. Its next vision is more ambitious: to 10X GDP by 2050 (base year 2023).

Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurship, community engagement, and sustainable economic development in Bhutan.

Core Pillars

Innovation


Encouraging creative thinking, experimentation, and entrepreneurial mindsets to develop new solutions, improve existing processes, and create opportunities for growth and positive impact.

Collaboration


By connecting young Bhutanese creators with global networks, mentors, and partners, this program fosters collaboration. In the "GMC Immersion" phases, participants work in joint teams to tackle challenges across four core sectors: policy, commerce and entrepreneurship, and non-profits.

Resilience


His Majesty The King underscored that the success of this monumental national project relies on core values like discipline, adaptability, humility, and constant learning. Under the resilience pillar, Bhutanese youth are equipped to adapt dynamically and persevere through hardships to protect the nation's long-term economic prosperity.

Our Pathways

Innovate Pathway

Explore innovative business ideas and startup opportunities through workshops and mentorship. Forward-thinking, practical innovation programs dedicated to solving modern city-building issues and unlocking new, high-growth career pathways in the GMC.

Build Pathway

Gain practical entrepreneurial skills, leadership training, and startup development support while connecting with a vibrant network of industry mentors and like-minded innovators. Through hands-on workshops and collaborative projects, you will build the resilience and strategic mindset needed to thrive in today’s competitive market.

Sustain Pathway

Focus on sustainability, social impact, and long-term entrepreneurial success. By integrating responsible practices into core business strategies, tomorrow's leaders can spark ethical innovation, foster deep consumer trust, and build resilient companies capable of thriving in an evolving global economy.
