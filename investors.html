Why Global Investors Choose GMC

Driving innovation, sustainability, and investment opportunities in Bhutan.

01

Government-Backed Ecosystem

GMC operates within a stable and government-backed innovation ecosystem, providing investors with confidence, policy support, and long-term commitment toward entrepreneurship, technology development, and sustainable economic growth.

02

Untapped Markets

Investors gain access to emerging opportunities across Bhutan and the South Asian region, where innovative startups and growing industries offer significant potential for expansion, market leadership, and long-term value creation.

03

ESG Investment Focus

SGMC prioritizes environmental sustainability, social responsibility, and strong governance practices, helping investors align their portfolios with global ESG standards while supporting impactfuland responsible business ventures.

04

Investment-Friendly Policies

Supportive regulations, investor-oriented policies, and development initiatives create an attractive environment for both domestic and international investors seeking secure and growth-focused investment opportunities.

05

Startup Collaboration

GMC facilitates meaningful connections between investors, startups, incubators, mentors, and industry leaders, enabling strategic partnerships, knowledge sharing, and accelerated business growth.

06

High-Growth Enterprises

Investors receive early access to promising green technology, digital innovation, and scalable enterprises with strong growth potential, creating opportunities for substantial returns and long-term impact.
